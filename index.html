Vision

Why elioo

Most of us carry a phone and a thousand small worries. Did I take that pill. What did the doctor actually say. Where did the money go this month.

elioo builds small, friendly AI companions that take one worry each and hold it well. They live where you already are: WhatsApp, the browser, your meetings. They remember, they nag gently, and they never pretend to be a doctor or a bank.

This is a studio in Dhaka building for the next billion phones, one bot at a time.
